# Privacy Policy for Ukraine Metro

**Last updated:** November 22, 2026

**Ukraine Metro** is a non-commercial application developed by **Viktor Varenyk**. Your privacy is important, and this policy explains how the app handles your information.

## Data Collection and Tracking

* **No Personal Data:** The app itself does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

* **No Tracking:** The app does not use analytics, tracking tools, or user profiling.

* **Offline Usage:** All core map and station features work locally on your device without transmitting user data to external servers.

## Advertising

The app includes an optional ad banner provided by **Google AdMob**. AdMob may collect basic device identifiers or use cookies to serve ads in accordance with Google's privacy policy. 

You can disable this ad banner **for free** at any time in the app's settings.
